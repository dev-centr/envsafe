== Installer packages, v0.2.1

Packaging only; the binary behaves the same as v0.2.0.

* *macOS:* `brew install dev-centr/tap/envsafe` from the new formula in https://github.com/dev-centr/homebrew-tap[dev-centr/homebrew-tap], or a `.pkg` installer that puts `envsafe` in `/usr/local/bin`. The `.pkg` holds a universal binary when the Intel slice cross-compiles on the arm64 runner. That slice is untested on Intel hardware, and the `.pkg` is not signed or notarized.
* *Linux:* `.deb` and `.rpm` packages for x64 and arm64 (built with nfpm; binary in `/usr/bin`). CI installs and removes the `.deb` on both architectures.
* *Archives:* the `.tar.gz` and `.zip` files remain for packagers. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers every asset.
* *Tap bump:* the release workflow updates the formula when the `HOMEBREW_TAP_TOKEN` secret is set; otherwise follow `packaging/homebrew/README.md`.
